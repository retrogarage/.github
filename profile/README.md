  # Welcome to Retro Garage

  **A workshop for bringing old ideas back to life and building new ones with a retro twist.**
</div>

Retro Garage is where I, [Fulvio](https://github.com/Fulviuus), create and share:

- 🕹️ Retro computing and gaming experiments
- 🔧 FPGA cores and tools for [MiSTer](https://misterfpga.org/)
- 💾 Hardware, software, and preservation projects
- ⚡ Prototypes, curiosities, and whatever else rolls onto the workbench

Projects here may range from early experiments to polished releases. If you enjoy classic hardware, FPGA development, or creative retro tech, take a look around.
